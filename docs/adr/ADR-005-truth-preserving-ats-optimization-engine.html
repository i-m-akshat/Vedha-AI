ADR-005 ACCEPTED 2026-10-02

Truth-Preserving ATS Optimization Engine & Metric Invariants

💡 Plain-English Summary: Why Does This Matter?

When people use generic AI chatbots to write resumes, the AI frequently lies. It might change "managed a $50,000 budget" into "managed a $5,000,000 budget", or invent that you graduated from Stanford when you didn't, just to score higher on an ATS keyword test. Getting caught lying on a resume ruins careers and gets candidates blacklisted by recruiters. Vedha AI solves this with Truth-Preserving Guardrails: our AI is mathematically prevented from inventing companies, degrees, or inflated numbers. If the AI hallucinates a metric, our verification engine catches it and automatically rolls it back to your real, verified numbers.

1. Context & The Problem

Standard generative AI models suffer from unconstrained hallucination when tasked with optimization. Common resume tailoring hallucinations include:

  • Fictitious Companies: Inventing previous employers to fill job description gaps.
  • Degree Inflation: Upgrading Bachelor's degrees to Master's degrees or claiming unearned certifications.
  • Quantitative Metric Inflation: Fabricating impact statistics (e.g. inventing "reduced latency by 90%" or "led 50 engineers" when the candidate led 4).

2. The Architectural Decision

We designed a four-stage defense-in-depth pipeline:

  • 1. Pre-Generation Constraints: The LLM system prompt explicitly prohibits generating unverified metrics, inventing past companies, or changing academic degrees. It enforces that 70–80% of bullet points preserve candidate-verified numbers.
  • 2. Post-Generation Invariant Checker (ValidateTruthPreservation):
    • Company Invariant: All companies in tailored.Experience must exist in master.Experience.
    • Institution Invariant: All schools in tailored.Education must exist in master.Education.
    • Metric Invariant: Regex scans all numbers, percentages (%), currencies ($), and multipliers (3x). Any metric not found in the candidate's master resume triggers an immediate violation.
  • 3. Auto-Rollback Remediation: If a bullet point violates a metric invariant, the engine automatically rolls back that specific bullet to the authentic master resume text instead of failing the user's request.
  • 4. Four-Pillar ATS Recruiter Scorecard: Calculates a realistic, actionable match score: Keyword Match (40%), Experience Relevance (25%), Skills Alignment (20%), and Formatting/Parsability (15%).

3. Alternatives Evaluated

❌ Unconstrained AI Rewriting

Achieves 98%+ keyword matches on paper, but invents qualifications. Unacceptable for serious candidates and fails human recruiter background checks.

❌ Hidden Keyword Stuffing

Embedding invisible white text or repeated tags is flagged and auto-rejected by modern parsing algorithms in Workday, Greenhouse, and Taleo.

4. Consequences & Impact

  • Rock-Solid Candidate Safety: Zero risk of submitting fabricated metrics or fake degrees.
  • Actionable Feedback: The recruiter scorecard explains exactly which keywords are present and which are missing so the candidate knows where they genuinely stand.
