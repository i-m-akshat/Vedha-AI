Architecture decisions · ADR-008

Ground LinkedIn screening answers, never guess

ACCEPTED Extension 2026-10-10
Easy Apply contact fields and employer quizzes are different. Name and email come from the profile. “Years with PostgreSQL”, “commute to Bangalore”, and “valid B1 visa” must come from a fact the candidate already verified. If that fact is missing, the field is highlighted for the candidate. The extension does not invent 5 years, a salary, or a visa answer to get past Next.

Context

LinkedIn renders screening prompts inside .fb-dash-form-element, fieldset legends, and div[role="combobox"], not as name="email" inputs. The previous filler matched broad substrings (“years”, “name”, any combobox) and backfilled constants when the profile was blank. That both blocked submission and filed inaccurate applications.

Decision

  1. Extract one question per control from the nearest shell that holds a single input or one radio group. Prefer legend and label spans over option labels.
  2. Resolve locally from approved answers, explicit profile booleans, skill-year maps, and resume roles whose text names the skill and whose dates yield a duration.
  3. Send only unresolved, non-identity questions to POST /api/orchestrator/generate-answers, including options and masterResumeId.
  4. Auto-fill only when confidence is at least 0.8 and, for radios and selects, the answer equals an option. Otherwise badge the control “Please review”.
  5. Keep human typing jitter (15–35ms) and pointer movement. Safe fill does not click Next.

Alternatives

OptionWhy it lost
Keep total years as the fallback for every “years” promptFiles the candidate’s whole career against a single tool and fails truth checks.
Always call the model firstAdds latency and a hallucination path for facts the profile already states exactly.
Leave blanks with no badgeThe candidate cannot see why Next is disabled.

Consequences

  • Safe auto-fill can pause on a step until the candidate confirms an unknown visa or commute city. That is intentional.
  • Skill years summed across roles can over-count overlapping jobs. The number is still derived from dated resume text, not a constant.
  • EEO questions (gender, race, veteran, disability) stay on the review path and are not sent to the model.

Implemented in extension/content.js (extractFormQuestions, resolveScreeningAnswer, fillModalInputs) and covered by tests/e2e/screening_questions.test.js.
